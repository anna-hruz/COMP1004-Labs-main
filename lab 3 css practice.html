Top 5 fun facts about orange cats

  • All orange/ginger cats are tabbies.
  • Ginger cats have M-like shape pattern on the top of their head.
  • In many cultures orange cats associated with fortune and good luck.
  • Only around 20% of orange cats are female.
  • Many orange cats develop harmless dark brown or black freckles (lentigo simplex) on their noses, lips, or gums as they age.
